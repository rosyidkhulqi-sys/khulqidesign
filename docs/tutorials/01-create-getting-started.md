# Tutorial 01 — Getting started with Create

- **Title on site:** Getting started with Create
- **Length:** about 6–7 minutes (≈ 900 words read at a calm pace)
- **Record:** 1920×1080, browser at 100% zoom, dark theme, logged in as a normal
  (not admin) account so the AI tab and admin menus don't show
- **Voice:** calm, friendly, not salesy. Read the **Say** lines; do the **Do** lines.
  Pause a beat after each click so viewers can follow.

## 0:00 — Welcome

**Do:** Start on the Khulqidesign Home page, then click **Create**.

**Say:**
Hi, and welcome to Khulqidesign. In the next few minutes I'll show you how to
make a finished design in Create, our editor that works right in your browser.
You don't need to install anything, and you can start without an account.
By the end, you'll know how to edit a template, add your own touches, and
download it ready to post or print.

## 0:30 — Start a design

**Do:** In the new design window, click **Instagram Post**. Then open the
**Templates** tab on the left, scroll a little, and click a template.

**Say:**
Every design starts with a size. Pick one of the ready-made sizes, like
Instagram Post, or type your own width and height.
On the left you'll find the Templates tab. Each template is made by our team,
and you can change everything in it — the text, the colors, and the pictures.
Let's pick this one. One click, and it's on the page.

## 1:15 — Edit text

**Do:** Double-click the headline, type a new one. Select it, change the font
from the font picker, then the size. Show bold, letter spacing and alignment
in the top bar.

**Say:**
To change the text, just double-click it and type.
When a text is selected, the bar at the top shows everything you can change:
the font, the size, bold and italic, letter spacing, line spacing and
alignment. Every font in Create is free to use in your designs, for personal
and commercial work.
Want to add more text? Open the Text tab and click a heading, a subheading or
body text.

## 2:15 — Colors, photos and backgrounds

**Do:** Select a shape, click its color, pick one from the panel, then use the
eyedropper. Drag a photo from **Uploads** onto a photo frame. Double-click the
photo to adjust its crop. Open **Background** and click one.

**Say:**
Select any element and click its color to change it. You can pick from the
palette, type a hex code, or use the eyedropper to take a color from your
design.
To use your own photos, open Uploads and drop your files in. Then drag a photo
onto a frame, and it fills the frame for you. Double-click it to move or crop
the picture inside.
And the Background tab gives you colors, gradients and ready-made backgrounds
for the whole page.

## 3:15 — Elements: icons, shapes and more

**Do:** Open **Elements**. Type "coffee" in the search box, drag an icon in.
Change its color. Open **Shapes**, add one and change its corners. Show
**Tables** and **QR code** briefly.

**Say:**
The Elements tab holds a growing library of pieces you can add: icons, vectors,
shapes, photo frames, tables, and even QR codes.
Search for what you need — let's try "coffee" — and drag it onto your design.
Icons and shapes stay sharp at any size, and you can recolor them in one click.
Shapes can change their corners and sides, tables keep your data neat, and a
QR code is made from any link you type.
Items marked Premium are part of a subscription. You can try them in your
design freely; they only need Premium when you download.

## 4:15 — Pages, layers and resize

**Do:** Click **Add page** under the artboard, then **Duplicate page**. Open
**Layers** and drag one layer above another; lock one. Open **Resize** and
choose a Story size.

**Say:**
A design can have many pages — great for carousels and presentations.
Add a new page, or duplicate this one to keep the same style.
The Layers tab shows everything on the page. Drag to change what sits in
front, and lock what you don't want to move by accident.
Need the same design in another size? Use Resize and choose a new size.
Create fits your layout to it, and backgrounds stretch to fill the new page.

## 5:15 — Animate

**Do:** Select the headline, open **Animate**, pick an effect, press play.

**Say:**
To bring a page to life, select an element and open Animate. Choose an effect,
and press play to preview it. Animated pages can be downloaded as a video or a
GIF.

## 5:45 — Download and your projects

**Do:** Click **Download**. Show PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF (and MP4, GIF for the
animated page). Choose PNG, download. Then open **Projects**.

**Say:**
When you're happy, click Download. PNG gives you the best quality, JPG a
smaller file, SVG a scalable vector, and PDF every page at print quality.
Animated pages can also be saved as MP4 or GIF.
The first time you download, we'll ask you to log in. A free account gives you
ten downloads a day.
Your work saves as you go — you'll see "Saved" at the top. Find all your
designs again any time in the Projects tab.

## 6:30 — Wrap-up

**Do:** Show the finished design full screen for a few seconds.

**Say:**
And that's it — from a template to a finished design in a few minutes.
Explore the templates, try your own colors and photos, and have fun creating.
Thanks for watching, and see you in the next tutorial.
